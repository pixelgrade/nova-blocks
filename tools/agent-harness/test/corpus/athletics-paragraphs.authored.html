About us

Athletics was named for our first office: a dusty, un-air-conditioned boxing gym in an earlier, grittier incarnation of Williamsburg. None of us boxed. But it captured our founders’ scrappy entrepreneurialism.

We’ve since grown to a larger team and a proper office with windows and HVAC. But the spirit of that boxing gym survives: in our work, our collaborative style, and the creative values we fight for every day.
